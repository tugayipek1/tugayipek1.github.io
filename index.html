About

I'm a full stack developer with 5+ years of experience building enterprise software, covering analysis, architecture, implementation and running it in production. Most of my work sits on React, Angular and Vue.js on the frontend and Spring Boot, Node.js and NestJS on the backend, backed by PostgreSQL, Oracle and Docker.

Currently I work at Phexum, where I build JoedTech, an EdTech platform for language institutes and educational institutions. Before that I spent time on ERP enhancements for Erdemir (OYAK Mining Metallurgy Group), an AI-supported cash-flow optimisation tool for a logistics company, and an NLP SaaS platform.

Day to day I use agentic development workflows with human-in-the-loop review, MCP-based tool integrations and event-driven automations, and I care about the unglamorous parts too: query performance, incident handling and end-to-end tests.
